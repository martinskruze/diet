Pork tenderloin with potatoes, green beans and mustard skyr

Rubbed roast pork tenderloin with boiled potatoes and green beans, with a mustard skyr pot. Makes 5 meal prep boxes.

Servings: 5 (1 box with a mustard skyr pot each)

Per serving: 685 kcal · protein 49 g · fat 11 g · carbs 100 g

Without the mustard skyr pot: 643 kcal, protein 42 g, fat 11 g, carbs 97 g per box.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

  • 700 g pork tenderloin, raw
  • 2500 g potatoes, raw
  • 750 g frozen green beans
  • 20 g olive oil
  • 2 tbsp BBQ dry rub
  • 300 g plain skyr
  • 30 g mustard

Method

  1. Oven to 220°C fan. Cut the potatoes into even chunks, skin on, into cold salted water. Bring to the boil and cook 15 to 18 min until a knife slides in.
  2. Add the green beans to the same pot for the last 4 min. Drain, let the steam off, then toss with half the oil and a pinch of the rub.
  3. Pat the pork dry and coat it with the rest of the oil and the rub. Roast 18 to 22 min, to 63°C inside.
  4. Rest the pork 10 min under foil, then slice.
  5. Mustard skyr: stir the skyr, mustard and a pinch of salt together and split it into 5 small pots.
  6. Each box gets a fifth of the pork, potatoes and beans. Add the mustard skyr pot cold after reheating.

Check after importing

The pork should show about 840 kcal in total (an entry near 120 kcal per 100 g raw) and the potatoes about 1,900 kcal. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 685 kcal, fix that line first.
