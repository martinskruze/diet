Pork and bean chilli with rice

Pulled pork chilli with kidney beans, lentils and corn, served with rice. Makes 5 meal prep boxes.

Servings: 5 (1 box, about 500 g chilli plus 1 rice portion each)

Per serving: 794 kcal · protein 54 g · fat 16 g · carbs 101 g

Chilli only, no rice: 514 kcal, protein 48 g, fat 15 g, carbs 39 g per box.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

  • 800 g pork shoulder, lean only, raw
  • 4 tsp BBQ dry rub
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • 200 g onion
  • 300 g red bell pepper
  • 30 g chili con carne seasoning mix
  • 800 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 480 g canned kidney beans, drained
  • 300 g canned lentils
  • 100 g canned sweet corn, drained
  • 400 g long grain white rice, uncooked

The pork is written as raw lean weight. The 2.04 kg shoulder minus the trimmed fat is about 1.8 kg, split into 9 equal cooked piles: 4 piles (800 g raw) go in the chilli and 5 piles (1,000 g raw) in the BBQ bowls. MyFitnessPal matches raw pork far better than pulled pork.

Rice is written as dry weight: 800 g dry makes 10 portions, so 400 g dry covers these 5 boxes.

Method

  1. Pulled pork (cooked once for both pork recipes): oven to 160°C (fan 150). Cut the thick outer fat off 2.04 kg pork shoulder, cut it into 4 to 5 chunks, rub all sides with 3 tbsp BBQ rub, and put it in a deep dish with 250 ml water, lid on. Bake 3 h, until a fork twists a chunk apart. Shred, bin the fat seams, skim the fat off the juices and stir about 100 ml juice back in. Split into 9 equal piles. This recipe uses 4 of the 9 piles.
  2. Rice (cooked once for both pork recipes): boil 800 g rice in plenty of salted water for about 12 min, drain, spread on a tray to cool within an hour, and split into 10 equal portions. This recipe uses 5 of the 10 portions.
  3. Heat the oil in a big pot, add the chopped onion and peppers, and cook 5 to 6 min on medium heat until soft.
  4. Stir in the chili con carne seasoning for 1 min.
  5. Add the tomatoes, beans, lentils, corn and 150 ml water. Simmer 20 min with the lid ajar, stirring now and then.
  6. Stir in the 4 pork piles and simmer 5 min. Taste before adding any salt.
  7. Ladle evenly into 5 boxes, about 500 g each, and add 1 rice portion to each.

Check after importing

The pork should come to about 1,200 kcal in total (an entry near 150 kcal per 100 g raw) and the rice about 1,400 kcal. If the rice shows about 500 kcal, MyFitnessPal picked cooked rice: switch it to uncooked. If your lentil can is not 300 g, use the weight on its label. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 794 kcal, fix that line first.
