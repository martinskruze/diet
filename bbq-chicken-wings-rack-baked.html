Rack-baked BBQ chicken wings

Crisp oven-baked chicken wings on a rack with a light tomato and honey BBQ glaze. The fat drips into the tray. Makes 7 portions.

Servings: 7 (1 portion, about 140 g cooked (6 to 7 pieces) each)

Per serving: 400 kcal · protein 39 g · fat 24 g · carbs 7 g

Ingredients

Makes 7 servings.

  • 1500 g chicken wings, raw, with skin
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp BBQ dry rub
  • 70 g tomato paste
  • 30 g honey
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp mustard

The wings line is raw weight with skin and bone. Pick an entry near 200 kcal and 14 g fat per 100 g raw.

Method

  1. Oven to 220°C fan. Put a rack over a foil-lined tray, so the fat drips away from the wings.
  2. Pat the wings very dry with paper towels. Toss them with the baking powder, salt and almost all of the rub (keep 1 tsp for the glaze).
  3. Spread them on the rack, skin up, with gaps between them. Bake 25 min, flip, then 20 min more until deep golden and 75°C inside.
  4. Glaze: stir the tomato paste, honey, vinegar, mustard, 1 tsp rub and 3 to 4 tbsp water until smooth.
  5. Brush the wings with the glaze and bake 5 min more.
  6. Pour off the fat in the tray and bin it. Weigh the cooked wings and split them into 7 equal portions.
  7. Air fryer instead: 200°C for 22 to 25 min in batches, shaking halfway, then glaze for 2 min.
