Turkey bolognese with pasta

Lean turkey mince bolognese with carrots and Mutti tomatoes over pasta, with parmesan. Makes 5 meal prep boxes.

Servings: 5 (1 box with 10 g parmesan each)

Per serving: 750 kcal · protein 54 g · fat 14 g · carbs 99 g

Without the parmesan: 711 kcal, protein 50 g, fat 11 g, carbs 99 g per box.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

  • 750 g turkey breast mince, raw
  • 600 g dry pasta
  • 800 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 200 g onion
  • 150 g carrot
  • 10 g garlic
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 50 g parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs

Pasta is written as dry weight. Pick a turkey mince entry from the breast (lean), not a 7% or 15% fat mince.

Method

  1. Oil, finely chopped onion and carrot into a big pot over medium heat for 6 to 7 min until soft. Add the garlic for 1 min.
  2. Add the turkey, break it up and cook 6 to 8 min until no pink is left.
  3. Add the tomatoes, herbs and 200 ml water. Simmer 20 min with the lid ajar, stirring now and then. Taste, then salt and pepper.
  4. Boil the pasta 1 min less than the pack says, since it softens again when reheated. Drain, don't rinse.
  5. Weigh the pasta and split it into 5 boxes. Weigh the sauce and split it the same way on top. 10 g parmesan on each.

Check after importing

The turkey should show about 860 kcal in total (an entry near 115 kcal and 22 g protein per 100 g raw) and the pasta about 2,100 kcal as dry weight. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 750 kcal, fix that line first.
