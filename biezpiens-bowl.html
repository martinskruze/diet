Biezpiens bowl with banana and honey

Low fat quark (biezpiens 0.5%) with banana, honey and cinnamon. A 2 minute snack.

Servings: 1 (1 bowl each)

Per serving: 280 kcal · protein 26 g · fat 1 g · carbs 45 g

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

  • 150 g quark, 0.5% fat
  • 120 g banana
  • 20 g honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Put the biezpiens in a bowl, slice the banana on top, and add the honey and cinnamon.

Check after importing

The quark should show about 110 kcal and about 24 g protein. If only full fat quark comes up, look for a 0.5% or low fat entry. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 280 kcal, fix that line first.
