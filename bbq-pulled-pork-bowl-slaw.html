BBQ pulled pork bowl with slaw

Pulled pork with rice, BBQ beans and corn, with a skyr mustard slaw on the side. Makes 5 meal prep boxes.

Servings: 5 (1 box plus 100 g slaw and 2 tbsp dressing each)

Per serving: 842 kcal · protein 60 g · fat 18 g · carbs 103 g

Without the slaw: 788 kcal, protein 55 g, fat 18 g, carbs 97 g per box.

Ingredients

Makes 5 servings.

  • 1000 g pork shoulder, lean only, raw
  • 5 tsp BBQ dry rub
  • 400 g long grain white rice, uncooked
  • 850 g barbecue baked beans
  • 200 g canned sweet corn, drained
  • 400 g white cabbage
  • 100 g carrot
  • 150 g plain skyr
  • 2 tbsp mustard

The pork is written as raw lean weight. The 2.04 kg shoulder minus the trimmed fat is about 1.8 kg, split into 9 equal cooked piles: 4 piles (800 g raw) go in the chilli and 5 piles (1,000 g raw) in the BBQ bowls. MyFitnessPal matches raw pork far better than pulled pork.

Rice is written as dry weight: 800 g dry makes 10 portions, so 400 g dry covers these 5 boxes.

Method

  1. Pulled pork (cooked once for both pork recipes): oven to 160°C (fan 150). Cut the thick outer fat off 2.04 kg pork shoulder, cut it into 4 to 5 chunks, rub all sides with 3 tbsp BBQ rub, and put it in a deep dish with 250 ml water, lid on. Bake 3 h, until a fork twists a chunk apart. Shred, bin the fat seams, skim the fat off the juices and stir about 100 ml juice back in. Split into 9 equal piles. This recipe uses 5 of the 9 piles.
  2. Rice (cooked once for both pork recipes): boil 800 g rice in plenty of salted water for about 12 min, drain, spread on a tray to cool within an hour, and split into 10 equal portions. This recipe uses 5 of the 10 portions.
  3. Pack 5 boxes, each with 1 pork pile, 1 rice portion, a fifth of the BBQ beans (about 170 g) and 40 g corn.
  4. Slaw: shred the cabbage and carrot finely and keep them in one box, undressed. Dressing: stir the skyr with the mustard, salt and pepper in a jar.
  5. On the day, reheat the box and add 100 g slaw and 2 tbsp dressing cold.

Check after importing

The pork should come to about 1,500 kcal in total, the rice about 1,400 kcal and the 2 cans of BBQ beans about 850 kcal. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 842 kcal, fix that line first.
