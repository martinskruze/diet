Tuna on rye bread

Two cans of tuna on two slices of rupjmaize with pickles and mustard. A 2 minute snack.

Servings: 1 (2 slices each)

Per serving: 397 kcal · protein 29 g · fat 15 g · carbs 33 g

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

  • 100 g canned tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 70 g rye bread
  • 50 g pickled cucumbers
  • 1 tsp mustard

Method

  1. Drain 2 cans of tuna well (about 100 g).
  2. Spread mustard on 2 slices of rupjmaize, add the tuna and the sliced pickles.

Check after importing

The tuna should be about 200 kcal. The expected numbers assume tuna in oil, drained. If your cans are in water, pick that instead and the total drops by about 85 kcal. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 397 kcal, fix that line first.
