Overnight oats jar with skyr

One overnight oats jar with skyr, berries and walnuts. Eat cold or warm after a morning session.

Servings: 1 (1 jar each)

Per serving: 630 kcal · protein 35 g · fat 17 g · carbs 81 g

Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

  • 80 g rolled oats
  • 150 g plain skyr
  • 140 ml skimmed milk 0.5%
  • 125 g frozen mixed berries
  • 15 g walnuts
  • 10 g honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Stir the oats, skyr and milk together in a jar.
  2. Put the frozen berries, walnuts, honey and cinnamon on top.
  3. Lid on, fridge overnight. Eat cold, or microwave for 2 min.

Check after importing

The oats should show about 300 kcal and the skyr about 95 kcal. Pick plain (unflavoured) skyr. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 630 kcal, fix that line first.
