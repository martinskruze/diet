Chicken tray with potatoes, broccoli and garlic skyr

Rubbed chicken thighs with potato wedges and broccoli, with a garlic skyr pot. Makes 4 meal prep boxes.

Servings: 4 (1 box with a garlic skyr pot each)

Per serving: 764 kcal · protein 57 g · fat 22 g · carbs 80 g

Without the garlic skyr pot: 726 kcal, protein 50 g, fat 22 g, carbs 77 g per box.

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

  • 800 g chicken thigh, boneless, skinless, raw
  • 1800 g potatoes, raw
  • 600 g frozen broccoli
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp chicken seasoning
  • 2 tsp all purpose seasoning
  • 240 g plain skyr
  • 1.5 tsp garlic seasoning

Method

  1. Oven to 210°C (fan 200). Trim the visible fat off the chicken.
  2. Tray 1: cut the potatoes into wedges, skin on, and toss them with the oil and the all purpose seasoning in a single layer.
  3. Tray 2: rub the chicken with the chicken seasoning and put the frozen broccoli around it.
  4. Roast both trays: chicken 25 to 30 min, until 75°C inside; potatoes about 35 min, turning once.
  5. Garlic skyr: stir the skyr with the garlic seasoning and split it into 4 small pots.
  6. Pack 4 boxes, each with a quarter of the chicken, potatoes and broccoli. Add the garlic skyr pot cold after reheating.

Check after importing

The chicken should come to about 1,100 kcal in total (an entry near 135 kcal per 100 g raw) and the potatoes about 1,350 kcal. If MyFitnessPal's per serving total is more than about 10% off 764 kcal, fix that line first.
